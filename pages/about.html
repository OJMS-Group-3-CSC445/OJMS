About the journal

The Journal of Applied Scholarship is an open-access publication for rigorous, original research and thoughtful exchange across disciplines. We support a transparent editorial process and make high-quality scholarship available to a broad global audience.

Editorial Board Reviewers

Aims and scope

  • Publish research that advances knowledge and practice.
  • Encourage responsible, inclusive, and transparent scholarship.
  • Connect researchers, practitioners, and communities across disciplines.
  • Support early-career authors through constructive editorial guidance.
